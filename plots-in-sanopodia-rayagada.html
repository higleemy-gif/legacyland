About the location

Why Sanopodia is worth the premium

Sanopodia sits in Kolnara, Rayagada District, and has been quietly picking up as a preferred residential address for families upgrading from town-centre flats to their own plot. What makes it work is the combination — a village-side setting for space and calm, with the day-to-day connectivity of Rayagada nearby.

A gated township in this pocket does two things for a buyer. One, it protects the value of every plot inside the layout because standards stay consistent. Two, it makes the neighbourhood safer and easier to live in — internal roads, plot boundaries, a real gate at the entry.

  • Gated entry with compound wall — security and privacy by design.
  • Planned internal roads — no fighting neighbours over encroachment.
  • Consistent plot standards — the layout stays looking good long-term.
  • Village-side setting inside Rayagada District, calm and residential.
  • Plot sizes from 18 × 50 ft onwards — enough room for a proper family home.
