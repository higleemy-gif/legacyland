About the location

Why Kolnora is a smart residential pick

Kolnora Tahasil sits inside Rayagada District with reliable road access into the town centre. The area has quietly grown around MIT's College (Majhighariani Institute of Technology and Science), which anchors long-term rental demand and gives the neighbourhood a young, family-oriented character.

For a plot buyer, that means two things you actually want — consistent access to daily amenities (grocery, transport, schools), and a location that keeps appreciating because education hubs almost always pull residential demand around them.

  • Directly opposite MIT's College — walkable to campus.
  • Motorable road access from the Rayagada city side.
  • Mixed residential-and-education neighbourhood — safer, calmer than industrial pockets.
  • Steady rental demand from students and college staff.
  • Plot sizes suited to both compact family homes and future expansion.
