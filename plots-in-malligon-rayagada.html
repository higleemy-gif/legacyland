About the location

Why buyers pick Malligon over the town centre

Malligon Village and the Kotapeta side of Rayagada District offer something the town centre can't — space, quiet, and honest land economics. Plots here are priced at what a genuine buyer can afford, layouts are wider, and neighbours are the kind you'd expect from a village-first community.

Connectivity into Rayagada town is via motorable road, so daily commutes for work or school are practical. And because the area hasn't been fully urbanised yet, land values still have real headroom to grow over the next few years.

  • Village-first community — safer, calmer than roadside town pockets.
  • Practical road access into Rayagada town.
  • All-facing plot layouts — pick your orientation.
  • Standard 15×60 and 18×50 ft sizes suited to family homes.
  • Entry pricing that's genuinely affordable for first-home buyers.
